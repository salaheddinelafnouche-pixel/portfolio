01 · Empathy & Discovery

The friction of sensory translation in e-commerce.

Modern fragrance commerce is broken. We buy scents based on external visual branding, expensive celebrity endorsements, or static profiles rather than olfactory truth.

Traditional scent discovery quizzes yield a 60% abandonment rate. The cause is a major translation void: users cannot map qualitative personal memories or desired feelings to quantitative chemical note descriptions (such as chypre, aldehydes, or vetiver).

YouScent was developed to bridge this gap. We conceptualized an AI-powered translation engine that allows users to express their identity in natural, personal language, mapping stories directly to structured fragrance compositions.

Deconstructing the Olfactory Relationship.

To build a meaningful translation layer, we conducted semi-structured interviews with 12 participants across four curated archetypes.

User Segments & Criteria

Segment Profile Recruitment Criteria
Scent Enthusiasts Fragrance collectors, community members Owns 10+ bottles, follows communities
Emotional Seekers Mood/feeling-oriented selectors Selects products based on emotion, wellness
Identity Crafters Curators of meticulous personal brand High value on personal presentation
Gift Givers Value the story over the generic object History of buying deeply personal gifts

Core Interview Protocol Questions

A. Relationship: "When you think of your earliest memory involving a specific smell, what comes to mind?"
B. The Selection Struggle: "When you read fragrance descriptions online (bergamot, white musk), do those words help you predict the smell?"
C. Emotional Vocabulary: "If you could design a scent capturing who you are right now, what textures, temperatures, or times of day would it evoke?"
D. Trust & AI: "If the AI explained *why* it chose each ingredient based on your narrative, would that change your trust?"
The Defined Problem
"People seeking a truly personalized scent are trapped between their intangible memories and the gatekept language of fragrance chemistry, turning what should be an intimate emotional journey into an anxious transaction of guesswork."

Positioning the Opportunity

YouScent occupies the only quadrant combining emotional inputs with generated unique formulations. Users do not need to choose notes; their story operates as the formula blueprint.
